**Start Date:** 2024  
**Status:** Ongoing  
**Description:** Development of scalable integrated photonic platforms for telecommunications and sensing applications.

**Key Focus:**
- Silicon photonic waveguides
- Passive and active optical components
- Chip-scale integration

**Collaborators:** MIT, industry partners
