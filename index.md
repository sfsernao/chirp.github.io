## Welcome to CHIRP

The **CHIRP (Collaborative Human-Integrated Research in Photonics)** group at Bridgewater State University focuses on cutting-edge photonic device design, optical systems, and advanced materials characterization.

**Directed by:** Prof. Samuel F. Serna Otálvaro

### Research Focus Areas
- **Photonic Device Fabrication** - Advanced manufacturing techniques
- **Optical Characterization** - State-of-the-art measurement systems
- **Integrated Photonics** - Silicon photonics and Chalcogenide platforms
- **Applied Materials Science** - Novel photonic materials
